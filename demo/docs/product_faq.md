# Product FAQ

## Ridgeline 2 tent

The Ridgeline 2 is a two-person backpacking tent weighing 1.9 kg with a 3,000 mm waterproof rating on the fly.

Replacement tent poles are free within the two-year warranty; order them from the support portal.

## Summit hiking boots

Summit boots run half a size small, so we recommend ordering half a size up.

Break the boots in for about 20 km of easy walking before a long trek.

## Warranty

All Northpeak gear has a two-year warranty against manufacturing defects.

The warranty does not cover normal wear, misuse or damage from fire.

## Down jacket care

Wash down jackets on a gentle cycle with down-specific soap and tumble dry on low heat with dryer balls.
